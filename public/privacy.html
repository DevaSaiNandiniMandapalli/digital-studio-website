Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

This policy explains what information this website collects, why it is collected and how it is handled. By using this website you agree to the practices described below.

Information you share with us

When you contact us — through the enquiry form, WhatsApp, phone or email — you may share your name, business name, phone number, email address, existing website address and details about your project. We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a proposal and deliver the work you ask for.

How the enquiry form works

This website is a static site with no server-side database. When you submit the enquiry form, your details are formatted into a message that opens in WhatsApp so you can review it and press send. Nothing is transmitted to us until you send that message. If an enquiry endpoint is configured for this deployment, the details are sent directly to our email inbox instead.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies and does not run third-party tracking scripts by default. If analytics are added in future, this policy will be updated and, where required, consent will be requested before any non-essential cookie is set.

Third-party services

Messages sent through WhatsApp are handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy. Portfolio links open websites that we designed and built for clients; those sites have their own policies that we do not control.

Data retention

Enquiries are kept only as long as needed to respond and, where a project proceeds, to maintain the working relationship and meet legal or accounting requirements. You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have shared with us. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable period.
